# Requirements matrix

[Português](requirements.md)

Each requirement has the phase in which it is first delivered and the evidence that allows marking it as accepted. Without the evidence, the requirement stays open.

This page is about requirements. The state of each syscall, of each service operation and of each shell command, with the test case that exercises it, is in `api-conformance.en.md`. The results of the acceptance runs are in `docs/acceptance/`.

| ID | Requirement | Phase | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | The image starts through the pinned OpenSBI, without a guest kernel and without semihosting | 1 | Scenario `boot` of `kernel-tool test --suite bootstrap`; `kernel-tool verify-artifacts` confirms a static ELF, without an undefined symbol |
| R-02 | Frozen Python policies run in S-mode with a fixed heap | 1 | `policy:` lines of the `boot` scenario; scenario `runtime-selftest` |
| R-03 | The collector preserves roots in registers and stacks | 1 | Native `gc-roots` probes of the `runtime-selftest` scenario |
| R-04 | A Python program enters U-mode through the user runtime and returns by trap | 1 | `init:` lines of the `boot` scenario, followed by `policy: reaped=init reason=exit value=0` |
| R-05 | The shared contracts behave the same on CPython and on the target MicroPython | 1, full corpus in 5 | Digests of the 10,000 transitions of the corpus compared in the `runtime-selftest` scenario, and `pytest tests/conformance` on CPython. Both run in `kernel-tool verify --all`; accepted differences in `runtime-differences.en.md` |
| R-06 | The DTB is validated with limits before any use | 1, devices in 3 | `kernel-tool native-check`, with `device_tree_test.c` and `device_inventory_test.c`; scenarios `device-tree-too-large` and `device-tree-without-memory` |
| R-07 | A boot failure and an unexpected trap produce a diagnostic and a final status | 1 | Scenarios `insufficient-memory`, `incompatible-segment`, `user-fault` and `supervisor-trap` |
| R-08 | A clean environment reproduces the image from recorded versions and hashes | 1, closed in 5 | `kernel-tool reproduce-build`: two builds in empty directories, equal to each other and to the current build, with the result in `qualification/reproduction.json`; `kernel-tool verify-artifacts --reproduce`, which also fails an image carrying the path of a directory of the tree, of the build or of the toolchain |
| R-09 | Generational identity: a released handle never becomes valid again | 1 in the model, 2 in the supervisor | `tests/conformance`, 10,000 transitions with invariants; `tests/native/object_table_test.c` and `capability_test.c`; suite `capabilities` |
| R-10 | Process selection by priority, round-robin and aging | 1 in the model, 2 with preemption | `tests/conformance`; suite `scheduling`, with the scheduling trace of the boot |
| R-11 | Atomic resource accounting per process and global | 1 in the model, 2 in the supervisor | `tests/conformance`; quotas in the suites `memory` and `capabilities`; scenarios `spawn-failures` and `cycles` of the `processes` suite |
| R-12 | CI separates success, failure and unavailable infrastructure | 1 | Exit codes of `kernel-tool` in the workflow |
| R-13 | Sv39 MMU, frames, W^X and ownership | 2 | Suite `memory`; `tests/native/address_space_test.c` and `frame_pool_test.c` |
| R-14 | Preemptive processes with full context, including FP | 2 | Suite `processes`: a loop that does not cooperate preempted and ended, integer and FP registers preserved between processes |
| R-15 | Capabilities, IPC and quotas enforced by the supervisor | 2 | Suites `capabilities` and `ipc`; `tests/native/message_test.c` |
| R-16 | Hard IRQ without Python, timer and safe GC | 1 and 2 | Timer path described in ADR 0004; scenarios `supervisor-trap` and `runtime-trap`; intensive collection preempted in the `processes` suite; external interrupts served only by native handlers, in `specs/abi/devices.en.md`. The pauses of the supervisor collector are measured by the supervisor itself and checked against the budget in `kernel-tool benchmark`; `tests/native/pause_statistics_test.c` |
| R-17 | Mediated DMA, VirtIO and device reset | 3 for block, entropy and console; network in 4 | Suite `devices`; `kernel-tool fault-test --suite device-reset`; `tests/native/virtio_queue_test.c` and `virtio_device_test.c`. For `virtio-net`, suite `network` and the `device-reset` case of `kernel-tool fault-test --suite network-faults` |
| R-18 | VFS and persistent volume with an `fsync` contract | 3 | Suites `vfs` and `storage`; `kernel-tool fault-test --suite storage-crash`, with the crash matrix, 1,000 generated sequences and the replay of the write log; `kernel-tool verify-filesystem --corpus tests/faults/storage`; `tests/conformance/test_filesystem_*.py` |
| R-19 | IPv4 networking, ICMP, UDP and TCP | 4 | Suite `network`; suite `network-interop`, with transfers in both directions checked by hash against the Linux TCP/IP stack, on a clean link and on a link with loss, duplication and reordering; `kernel-tool fault-test --suite network-faults`, with 31 malformed specimens, 32 forged connection requests, link down and device reset; `tests/conformance/test_network_link.py`, `test_network_reassembly.py`, `test_network_tcp.py` and `test_network_malformed.py`. Results in `docs/acceptance/network-and-operational-environment.en.md`. The fuzzing of the parsers is the `network` family of `kernel-tool fuzz`, with the targets `frames`, `packet-layers` and `stream-dialogue`; the required duration is in R-23 |
| R-20 | Shell, configuration and service supervision | 2 to 4 | Suites `applications`, `supervision` and `system-journey`, the last with two boots over the same volume; `tests/conformance/test_configuration.py`, `test_service_supervision.py`, `test_control_protocol.py` and `test_shell_words.py`. Each command and each operation has its line in `api-conformance.en.md` |
| R-21 | Provenance, licenses and distribution | Base in 1, closed in 5 | `kernel-tool inventory`; `sbom.spdx.json` and `licenses.json` of the bundle assembled by `kernel-tool release-check`; gate `dependencies`, with `config/release/advisories.toml`; `tests/host/test_release_inventory.py` |
| R-22 | ABI, service protocol, images, configuration and persistent format frozen at version 1.0, with rules of evolution | 5 | `specs/compatibility.en.md`; `specs/frozen-interfaces.toml`; `kernel-tool verify-interfaces`; `tests/host/test_frozen_interfaces.py` |
| R-23 | Fuzzing of the boot, interface, storage and network inputs, with a versioned corpus and a regression for each fixed failure | 5 | `kernel-tool fuzz`, `replay-corpus` and `promote-corpus`; `tests/fuzz/corpus/`; accumulated time per target in `build/fuzz/ledger.json`. The gate asks for 24 hours accumulated in each family |
| R-24 | Budgets of boot, memory, IPC, ready-task response, collector pauses and release after exit measured on the pinned profile | 5 | `kernel-tool benchmark`, with `qualification/measurements.json`; values in `docs/operations/reference-machine.en.md` |
| R-25 | Repeated boot, process cycles and soak without failure and without persistent resource growth | 5 | `kernel-tool repeat-boot --count 1000`; scenario `cycles` of the `processes` suite, with 10,000 cycles; `kernel-tool soak`. The gate asks for 24 hours in one boot |
| R-26 | Release bundle with images, manifest, checksums, SBOM, license inventory and a verifiable signature | 5 | `kernel-tool release-check`, `release-sign` and `release-verify`; `tests/host/test_release_bundle_files.py` and `test_release_inventory.py` |
| R-27 | First initialization, update, compatible rollback and recovery of the existing volume | 5 | Suite `release-journey`; `kernel-tool install`, `update` and `rollback`; `tests/host/test_release_installation.py`; `docs/operations/installation.en.md` |
| R-28 | Privileged boundary reviewed, security advisories of the dependencies handled and a vulnerability response channel | 5 | `docs/security/privileged-boundary-review.en.md`; `config/release/advisories.toml`; `SECURITY.en.md` and `docs/security/vulnerability-response.en.md` |
| R-29 | A release is only declared qualified with every gate met for the same images | 5 | `kernel-tool release-check`, with `release-report.json`; `tests/host/test_qualification_gates.py`; ADR 0010 |
